# Privacy Policy

**YouTube Hidden Banner** — Chrome extension

_Last updated: 2026-09-06_

## Summary

This extension does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

## Details

- **No data collection.** The extension does not gather any information about you, your browsing, or your activity.
- **No tracking or analytics.** There are no analytics, telemetry, cookies, fingerprinting, or third-party tracking of any kind.
- **No accounts.** No sign-in or registration is required or offered.
- **No network requests.** The extension makes no external calls. It contains no remote code and no JavaScript at all — all logic ships in the bundled `hide-banner.css` file.
- **Local only.** The extension runs exclusively on `youtube.com`, where it hides the statement/promo banner ad via CSS. Nothing leaves your browser.
- **Permissions.** The only permission requested is host access to `youtube.com`, used solely to apply the hiding rule on YouTube pages.
